Small questions, clear answers

Where do I find these numbers?

Ad spend comes from the ad account. Calls and forms come from call tracking, your inbox or CRM. Contacted, booked, showed and closed come from the phone log, booking calendar and invoices. Keep every number inside the same date range.

What if I only know leads and jobs closed?

Use those two numbers in Move 1. Put the same lead count in contacted, booked and showed. You will still get a correct overall conversion rate, CPL and CAC. The middle-stage rates will only become useful once you start tracking those steps separately.

Why can the calculator not discover my margin from ad data?

Ads know spend, leads and conversions. They do not know your parts, labor or fuel costs. Use the margin helper in Move 3 with one average job. Never guess a margin just to complete a report.
